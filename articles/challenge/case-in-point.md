本期没有长文。

只有一首诗。

它读起来有点别扭。标点没错，单词没错，韵脚也没错，作者甚至愿意为它的语法负责。但你就是会觉得哪里不对劲，像是有人在这首诗里做了点手脚，然后假装什么都没发生。

诗是这样的：

```text
oNe filE, OnE Slot, oNe HOLe tO fiLL,
the cYcLe loOPs aND pAYs no BIlL.
THE gRId layS oUT LIkE CiTY stREetS —
SwiPe LeFT, swipE rIGHt, And paGES gReeT.

mONDAY hAULs the oPEN-sOuRcE BRicK,
WeDNeSday's ESsaY, lOnG And ThicK,
tHURSDaY HackERs CRaCK THe COdE,
FrIdAY's CHiPs CHaSe mOoNLIgHt's rOAD.
tuESdAy, SATURdAY — LeFt to AIr,
NOT FrOm SLOTH, bUT BrEathINg Space TO spArE.

tHe FRONTmATtER lAyS DoWN THe LAw,
MARKdOWn WRIteS aND WE SaY "Caw."
ONe cOMmIT SHoVED, thE maChINE WAkES,
neTLifY BUiLdS fOR gOODneSS' SAKeS.
```

答案的格式是 `CaraFloa{...}`。

至于它藏在哪儿——这首诗已经把话说完了。
